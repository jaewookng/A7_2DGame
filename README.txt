The game starts as soon as the solution builds! Waves of slimes are attacking the player - you - the samurai.
The number of enemies will increase by wave and will spawn increasingly faster.
The player can be controlled by WASD characters (space = W) and left-click to attack with the sword.
Enemies will damage the player when coming in contact. Cut them down before they get too close!
The game may end naturally in two ways: victory or loss. The win condition is defeat of all enemies and surviving every wave.
The lose condition will trigger when the player has lost all 5 health bars. Falling off an edge will trigger death.
The game may be paused, ended, or restarted using the top-right HUD.
